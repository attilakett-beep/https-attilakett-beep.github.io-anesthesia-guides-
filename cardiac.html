    Before you begin

    Educational prototype — not clinical software

    This is an independently written demonstration of the layout and broad sequence of the preoperative cardiovascular evaluation workflow. It is not a validated implementation of the full guideline, and it does not reproduce all recommendations, indications, or exceptions.

    Do not use to decide whether to proceed with surgery, delay surgery, order tests, or treat a patient. A licensed clinician must apply the full current guideline and institution-specific policies.

    Do not enter names, MRNs, dates of birth, or other identifying information. Choices remain in memory and are discarded on page reload. The app works offline; guideline links require internet if opened.
