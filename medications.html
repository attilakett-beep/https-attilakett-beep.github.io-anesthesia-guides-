Clinical references & institutional review

Original, locally customizable rule candidates informed by independent guidance. References are broad supporting guidance and have not been independently cross-checked against every sentence in this prototype. No content or medication database was copied from the preoperative reference website.

Why this is not an automatic “hold / continue” order set: clinical indications, formulation, dose, urgency, local formulary, regional technique, kidney function, and postoperative plans all alter management. Some professional recommendations conflict; those rules are labeled for review.
