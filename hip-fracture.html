2a NT-proBNP → selective troponin plan

Proposed — committee review
Awaiting NT-proBNP result. No biomarker-based instruction to delay the OR.

Proposed follow-up: document a preoperative baseline troponin if feasible without surgical delay; postoperative surveillance at approximately 24 and 48 hours per approved pathway. An abnormal postoperative troponin requires same-day medical review (symptoms, ECG, vitals, hemoglobin and alternative causes) and escalation for suspected acute ischemia or instability. Geriatrics/medicine is the proposed owner. Clinical indications can require testing regardless of NT-proBNP.

The 300 ng/L reference reflects CCS risk stratification, not a locally validated hip-fracture cutoff. No automatic diagnosis, treatment, clearance or delay.
