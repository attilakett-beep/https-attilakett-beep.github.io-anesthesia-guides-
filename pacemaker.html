    Device & magnet quick reference

    Manufacturer and model behavior varies. These are device categories, not a substitute for the manufacturer's current instructions or device interrogation.

    Pacemaker / CRT-P

    A verified magnet response may cause asynchronous pacing; it can be disabled, brief, or manufacturer-specific. In pacing-dependent patients exposed to meaningful EMI, discuss asynchronous pacing via reprogramming or a validated magnet strategy.

    Transvenous ICD / CRT-D

    Magnet generally suspends tachyarrhythmia detection/therapy without changing the pacing mode. If pacing-dependent and exposed to EMI, a magnet alone does not solve pacing inhibition; device-team reprogramming is typically needed.

    Subcutaneous / extravascular ICD

    Defibrillator therapies can require temporary suppression in relevant EMI cases. Verify magnet response and positioning. S-ICD has no routine bradycardia pacing. EV-ICD capabilities differ; involve the device team.

    Leadless pacemakers

    Medtronic Micra: no magnet response. Abbott AVEIR: may have a magnet-triggered asynchronous response, but it can be hard to activate reliably. Verify the exact model and plan.

    Implantable loop recorder

    A monitor records rhythms but does not pace or deliver shocks. Usually there is no pacing or defibrillation reprogramming need; confirm that the device was correctly identified and assess proximity-related concerns.

    EMI mitigation

    Use bipolar or ultrasonic techniques when feasible. For monopolar electrosurgery, minimize energy and burst duration and route current away from generator/leads. Underbody return pads can change risk even below the umbilicus.

    Approval checklist

    Complete with electrophysiology/device clinic, anesthesia leadership, and perioperative nursing before clinical rollout.

    □ Confirm institutional device interrogation currency policy and urgent-case escalation contacts.
    □ Validate local magnet policy and manufacturer/device-model exception matrix.
    □ Review leadless, CRT, subcutaneous, extravascular, abdominal-generator, and nonstandard-device rules.
    □ Define who performs reprogramming, where, and how documentation is shared.
    □ Establish external pacing/defibrillator and pad placement workflow.
    □ Define postoperative ICD therapy reactivation ownership and handoff.
    □ Test high-risk cases and implementation against device-team-approved scenarios.
    Scope: Noncardiac procedures and common EMI decisions. This does not implement the specialized protocols for MRI, radiation therapy, cardioversion, catheter ablation, or other device-interacting procedures.
