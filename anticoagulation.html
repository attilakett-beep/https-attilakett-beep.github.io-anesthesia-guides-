Block classification guide

Illustrative block categories drawn from the 2022 ESAIC/ESRA classification and the 2025 ASRA deep-block rule. Always verify approach, depth, vascularity and compressibility locally.

Hip fracture application: the ED analgesia pathway can call for immediate anesthesia assessment and an appropriate block without awaiting routine laboratory results by default. That does not mean PENG is automatically appropriate in an anticoagulated patient. A fascia iliaca technique and a PENG technique must be assessed separately.

Regional anesthesia lead and pharmacy must verify every hold, restart and catheter rule against licensed guideline access.

Block taxonomy

Approve approach-specific categorizations for PENG, fascia iliaca, truncal blocks and catheter techniques.

Renal/assay rules

Define acceptable CrCl methods, drug-specific assays and quality/turnaround controls.

Operations & exceptions

Define urgent cases, concurrent antithrombotics, loading doses, traumatic puncture, catheter escalation, and neurologic monitoring.

Scope: This guide covers the major systemic antithrombotic classes but is not a complete ASRA implementation. Rare drugs, special populations (including obstetrics), continuous infusions, drug interactions, and complex restart scenarios require specialist review. An unlisted drug must never be treated as no anticoagulation. The guideline itself is the definitive source.
